Slide Saver

Save a SlideShare presentation as a PDF, a PowerPoint file or images.

Paste the presentation's link, check the slides, and download them. Nothing to install, no account needed.

Works with public presentations on slideshare.net.

Click a slide to leave it out of the download. Save one slide on its own with the link under it.

    How to download from SlideShare

    1. Copy the link

      Open the presentation on slideshare.net and copy the address from your browser's address bar or the share menu.

    2. Paste it here

      Paste the link in the box above and select Show slides. Every slide appears as a preview.

    3. Pick a format

      Choose PDF for reading and printing, PowerPoint to present, or a ZIP of images to reuse single slides.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit on the number of presentations.

    Can I edit the PowerPoint file?

    Each slide is placed as a full-size picture, so the layout matches the original exactly. You can add text and shapes on top, but the original text is part of the picture.

    Why did my link not work?

    The presentation may be private, deleted, or not a SlideShare presentation page. Open the link in your browser to check that it shows the slides, then copy it again.

    What quality are the downloads?

    Slide Saver takes the largest image SlideShare offers for each slide, usually 2048 pixels wide.

    Can I download only some slides?

    Yes. After the previews load, click the slides you don't want to remove them from the download.

    Is downloading allowed?

    Only download presentations you have the right to use, such as your own work or slides whose author allows reuse. Respect the author's copyright and license.
